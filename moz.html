MOZ

Director of Design & UX

At Moz I led the creative department: a team of 6 covering everything UX and design for the company, across both product and marketing. The team included a Creative Director, a UX Architect, two UX Designers, a Motion Designer, and a Graphic Designer.

I set the direction, and often detailed ideas, for the work below, while most of the hands-on design was done by my very talented team. I also put in place the design process the team worked by, including weekly design meetings, one focused on projects and one on innovation.

Moz Local

Branding, Home Page, and First Experience

We created the product branding and home page for Moz Local.

As with every Moz product, a lot has to happen before data is gathered and insights appear. We used several ways to bridge that gap, from introductions and setup wizards to inline messages for sections without data yet and email notifications. And, as always at Moz, each experience had just the right level of humor and fun.

Moz Analytics

Dashboards and Setup

For the dashboard redesign we explored an edgy concept, then only partly went that route, scaling back the more experimental data visualization to focus on comprehension.

Users needed data and actionable insight as quickly as possible, so we greatly improved the setup wizard to gather all the necessary data from them early.

Process

Mini-Wires

We came up with the concept of mini-wires: very lightweight wireframes with just enough detail to iterate with every discipline on task flow and information architecture, but not so much that reviews turn into discussions of small UI details. The UX Strategist on the team evolved them into a very useful tool.

Brand & Marketing

Marketing Materials

About half of my team created a large volume of marketing materials, from ad banners, sites, and whitepapers to physical things like clothing and dolls of Roger, the Moz mascot. The team even contributed significantly to the interior design of the new Moz office.
